### 🧩 Problems I've solved

| The business had | I built | Today |
|---|---|---|
| Quotes in loose Word and PDF files | A quotation manager that reads PDF and Word quotes with AI and loads them on its own, marks in red the ones with no news for days, and drafts the follow-up message | In use by the sales team of an industrial company |
| Time-clock data trapped in an old text-screen system | Upload the sheet and a dashboard shows lateness and absences by sector, shift and person, and flags the rows to review | In use at a metalworking plant |
| Every invoice checked by hand against the tax authority export before filing VAT | An agent that reads purchase invoices (PDF), cross-checks them against the export (ARCA, Argentina's tax authority) and flags each difference, with a plain-language summary for the accountant | Delivered; with real data it found differences |
| An HR review method that only existed as a prototype | An app where HR launches review campaigns and each leader evaluates their own team, with a signed document per person | In use by the HR team of an industrial company |
| Bookings and cash kept in a notebook | Online booking, plans, commissions and cash closing | In use at a barbershop and a car wash; live demo for padel complexes |
